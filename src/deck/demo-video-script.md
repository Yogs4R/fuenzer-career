# Fuenzer Career — Demo Video Script (3 Minutes)

> **Target:** Max 3 minutes. Simple English for beginner speaker.
> **Pacing:** Slow and clear. Pause at `[pause]` marks.
> **Screen:** Shows what to record for each section.

---

## 🎬 HOW TO RECORD THIS VIDEO

**What you need:**
- Record your screen + your face (small camera in corner is fine)
- Or just record your screen with voice-over
- Open the app in your browser before you start
- Have the Dashboard page ready on screen

**Voice tips:**
- Speak S-L-O-W-L-Y. Slower than you think is normal.
- Take a breath at every `[pause]`
- If you make a mistake — just stop, take a breath, and say the line again
- Don't worry about perfect accent. Clear and slow is better than fast.

## 🎬 RECORDING SETUP CHECKLIST

- [ ] Browser open on Dashboard (`/`)
- [ ] Close all other tabs (less distraction)
- [ ] Good lighting on your face (window in front, not behind)
- [ ] Microphone not too close to your mouth
- [ ] Drink water before you start
- [ ] Do a 10-second test recording first
- [ ] Have your job role ready to type (e.g., "Frontend Developer")
- [ ] Have a short answer ready to speak (2-3 seconds is fine)

---

## 💡 EXTRA TIPS

1. **You don't need to use my exact words.** If a sentence feels too long, make it shorter. Use your own style.

2. **The first take is always bad.** Do 2-3 takes. Pick the best one. Everyone does this.

3. **You can record voice separately.** Record the screen first. Then record your voice while watching the screen video. This is easier.

4. **Add simple subtitles.** In your video editor, add your words as text at the bottom. This helps viewers understand you better.

5. **Show your face for 10 seconds at the start.** Then switch to full screen. This makes the video more personal.

6. **Background music (very quiet).** Add soft background music at 10-15% volume. It covers small audio mistakes.
