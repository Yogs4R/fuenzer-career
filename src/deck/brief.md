## Problem
75% of job seekers feel nervous or unprepared before interviews. Mock interviews are expensive ($100+/hour), most practice tools are text-only (no voice), and there's no feedback on filler words like "um", "uh", "like".

## Solution
Fuenzer Career — your personal AI interview coach. Practice with your voice. Get instant feedback. Improve faster.

## Key Features
1. Voice-First Practice — Speak naturally. The app listens and transcribes in real time via Speechmatics.
2. AI-Powered Hints — STAR method suggestions for every question, generated by AI/ML API (deepseek-v4-flash).
3. Live Market Research — Bright Data scrapes real job listings from LinkedIn & Indeed for trending skills.
4. Filler Word Detection — Track every "um", "uh", "like" in real time.
5. Real-Time Transcription — See your words appear as you speak.
6. Guest-First Access — No sign-up required. Start practicing in under 10 seconds.

## How It Works
1. Market Research — Bright Data scrapes LinkedIn & Indeed → AI/ML API extracts trending skills
2. AI Interview (Voice) — You speak answers aloud → Speechmatics transcribes → Live filler word detection
3. Instant Evaluation — AI/ML API scores answers 0-100 → Skill match analysis → Delivery feedback
