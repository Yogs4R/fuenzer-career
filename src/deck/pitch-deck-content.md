## Slide 4 — How It Works (3 Steps)

**Layout:** Horizontal flow with arrows between steps.

**Text:**

```
Step 1: Market Research
Bright Data scrapes LinkedIn & Indeed for your target role
→ AI/ML API (deepseek-v4-flash) extracts trending skills

Step 2: AI Interview (Voice)
You speak your answers out loud
→ Speechmatics transcribes in real time
→ Live filler word detection ("um", "uh", "like")

Step 3: Instant Evaluation
AI/ML API (deepseek-v4-flash) scores your answers 0-100
→ Skill match analysis
→ Delivery feedback with tips
```

**Design tips:** 3 circles with numbers (1, 2, 3) connected by arrows. Use `#0369A1` for the circles. Below each circle: icon + description. Lucide icons: `search`, `mic`, `check-circle`.
